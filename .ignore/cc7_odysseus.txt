  ★ Insight ─────────────────────────────────────
  One Windows-specific correctness choice: I'll drive the subprocess with
  subprocess.Popen + a reader thread → asyncio.Queue, not
  asyncio.create_subprocess_exec. On Windows, asyncio subprocesses require the
  Proactor event loop, and uvicorn's loop choice isn't guaranteed — the
  thread-bridge pattern (which is exactly what Odysseus's own bg_jobs.py uses)
  sidesteps that entirely and reuses the codebase's platform_compat helpers.
  Robustness over elegance, matching house style.
